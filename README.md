# Jack Adams

Electrical engineering student at Auburn University interested in power
electronics, RF hardware, and PCB design.

## Featured Projects

### Formula SAE Boost Converter
Designed and routed a 12 V to 24 V boost converter in Altium Designer
for vehicle pumps and control electronics. The design targets up to 7 A
and has been released for fabrication.

### STM32 Handheld RF Remote
Drew the schematics and routed two custom PCBs in KiCad, including a
four-layer main board with 2.4 GHz RF and USB-C Li-ion charging.

### Formula SAE Shutdown Circuits
Designed comparator and RC timing circuitry for the BSPD and a flip-flop
latch and relay interface for the vehicle shutdown loop.
